# Manuel Rendon

Manuel Rendon is an inventor and the founder and CEO of Timeplast and String Cubed, two companies based in Winter Springs, Florida. An American born in San Cristobal, Venezuela, he trained as an environmental engineer at the Universidad Nacional Experimental del Tachira and later worked at PepsiCo as a national environmental coordinator, where he received a Best Practices Award.

He founded Timeplast in 2013 and filed his first U.S. patent application in 2014, for a composition for the degradation of plastic. Timeplast makes time-programmable, water-soluble materials, including the TimeMass line of functional 3D printing filaments. String Cubed, an analog computing company, works on programming matter so that everyday objects can sense, decide and act without electronics. It designs those filaments and the Bloominite seed pod. Rendon is the named inventor on eight granted U.S. patents. His work has been featured on CNN en Espanol and covered by Tom's Hardware, and Timeplast had a joint development relationship with Nestle Waters North America on its depolymerization technology (source: timeplast.com/about).
